## Slate Companion 0.5.1

> [!TIP]
> ### ⬇️ Downloads: these are the only files you need
> Easiest: **[devall96.github.io/SlateCompanion](https://devall96.github.io/SlateCompanion/)** picks the right one for your PC.
>
> - **Windows:** [**SlateCompanion-win-Setup.exe**](https://github.com/devall96/SlateCompanion/releases/download/v0.5.1/SlateCompanion-win-Setup.exe) (installer, no admin needed)
> - **Linux:** [**SlateCompanion.AppImage**](https://github.com/devall96/SlateCompanion/releases/download/v0.5.1/SlateCompanion.AppImage) (make it executable, then run it)
>
> Already on 0.5.0 or 0.4.x? Slate Companion offers this update when it opens.
>
> Everything else under *Assets* (`.nupkg`, `.json`, `RELEASES`) is read by the built-in updater: you can ignore it.

**The Play screen is back, slimmed down: Play and Clear Skies.**

### ▶️ Play is back
- 0.5.0 took the whole launcher out, and Clear Skies went with it. **The Play screen is back** (optional, as before: the first start asks, and Settings can hide it), now only for starting the game.
- **Play starts Savage Wind the normal way**, as if you opened it yourself: Savage Wind decides whether to show its Configuration screen or an update first. Slate Companion no longer skips past them.
- **Your copies of Savage Wind**: pick which one Play starts, rename it, open its folder, remove it from the list (nothing is deleted), or add a copy you have.

### ☁️ Clear Skies
- **Play with Clear Skies** again: its switch, the 20 looks, **Mods & GameBanana**, **Open Clear Skies**, and a note when Clear Skies has an update.

### 🚫 Still gone
- Installing, updating, setting up and going back between versions of Savage Wind, and Slate Companion's own copy of Savage Wind's settings. Savage Wind does all of that itself.
- 🐧 The app-menu entries for your Savage Wind AppImages are back, without the "Play (straight into the game)" shortcut; their **Savage Wind Configuration** shortcut stays.

> [!NOTE]
> Tested on Windows: every screen opens, the Play screen finds the test copy of Savage Wind and Clear Skies, and saves are read as before.
